Postfalcon now schedules posts to **Pinterest**, bringing the total to 9 live platforms:
Mastodon, Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Telegram, YouTube, TikTok, and Pinterest.

It connects the normal way &mdash; click **Connect** on the [Accounts](/accounts) page and go
through Pinterest's own OAuth login &mdash; using an access level that's open to any registered
app, not the partner-approval-gated tier Pinterest also has.

## Connecting Pinterest

A Pinterest Pin is always an image (there's no text-only Pin type), so every post to Pinterest
needs a photo attached. At connect time, Postfalcon picks your account's first board automatically
&mdash; there's no board-picker yet, so if you use multiple boards, all of your scheduled Pins
will land on that first one for now.

Postfalcon posts the image straight from its own upload URL, so there's no separate upload step;
the post's text becomes the Pin's description.

## It works everywhere else, too

Cross-posting, recurring posts, and the calendar all treat Pinterest like any other platform.
[Analytics](/blog/post-analytics-see-whats-working) is the one exception for now &mdash;
Pinterest has no metrics adapter yet, so its posts stay unmetered on the Analytics page, the same
honest gap Telegram and TikTok already have.

Head to [Accounts](/accounts) to connect it.
